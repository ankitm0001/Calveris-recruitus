15 / Questions you'll ask

The fair questions, answered.

Do we lose control?

No. You keep your systems, approvals and sign-off. We work inside them with access you grant and can revoke at any time.

How do we know the work is right?

Three sign-offs on every job, your rules in the client Brain, and ZRE so a fixed error stays fixed.

What if it doesn't work?

Start with one scope, such as billing for one division. You judge the pilot month before anything grows.

Will we have to keep explaining things?

Once. It goes into the Brain, so it survives holidays, sick days and staff changes.

Who do we talk to?

One named point of contact, plus a shared Teams channel with the team doing the work.

How fast can we start?

Ten working days from setup to your first close with us.
